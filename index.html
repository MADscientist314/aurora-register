Registration of interest

Research access to AURORA, and a consortium to train AURORA 2.0

AURORA is a convolutional neural network that scores routine 2D transabdominal ultrasound for placenta accreta spectrum (PAS). It was developed at Baylor College of Medicine and Texas Children's Hospital. We invite clinicians and scientists who want to evaluate it, and centers with pathology-confirmed PAS cases that want to help train the next version.

Ways to participate

Research use

Evaluate AURORA at your institution

  • A browser-based research viewer is in preparation; scans are processed on your own computer and never uploaded.
  • Access is granted to registered researchers under a research-only license.
  • Output is a continuous research score, not a diagnosis.
Data contribution

Help train AURORA 2.0

  • For centers with histopathology-confirmed PAS cases.
  • Images are de-identified at your site before anything is shared.
  • Participation runs under institutional IRB approval and signed data agreements.
  • Contributors are credited under a consortium authorship policy.
